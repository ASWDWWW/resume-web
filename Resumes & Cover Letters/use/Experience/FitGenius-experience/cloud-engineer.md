## What this role usually means

Cloud engineers implement services on a public cloud: IAM, functions, storage, networking, observability, cost. Depth varies from app-on-cloud to landing-zone specialist.

## What you actually did on FitGenius

FitGenius is a pre-launch consumer fitness product: iOS and Android (Expo / React Native), Firebase backend (~64 Cloud Functions, Firestore rules, IAP verification, Gemini AI), marketing site (Vite/React), app/legal/admin hosting, store-submission runbooks, and a legal/compliance suite. You were the founding technical partner — one person owning product, code, cloud, QA packets, and founder enablement from roughly May through September 2026.

GCP/Firebase production: Cloud Functions (Node 22, us-central1), Firestore, Storage, Hosting, Secret Manager, Vertex AI (Gemini, enterprise ADC), Pub/Sub for Play RTDN, App Check providers, Crashlytics, Remote Config, Analytics. Dual hosting targets and custom domains documented.

## How that work applies to **Cloud Engineer**

Strong for Firebase/GCP *application* cloud roles. Weak for AWS Solutions Architect / multi-account landing zone. You can talk least-privilege data (rules), secrets, scheduled jobs, and AI on Vertex.
