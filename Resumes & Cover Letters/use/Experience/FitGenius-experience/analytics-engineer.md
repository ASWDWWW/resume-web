## What this role usually means

Analytics engineers model data for business truth: dbt, metrics layers, semantic models, BI. They sit between DE and data analysts.

## What you actually did on FitGenius

FitGenius is a pre-launch consumer fitness product: iOS and Android (Expo / React Native), Firebase backend (~64 Cloud Functions, Firestore rules, IAP verification, Gemini AI), marketing site (Vite/React), app/legal/admin hosting, store-submission runbooks, and a legal/compliance suite. You were the founding technical partner — one person owning product, code, cloud, QA packets, and founder enablement from roughly May through September 2026.

You defined a 30+ event catalog, user properties (`plan_tier`, `onboarding_complete`), conversion events for funnels, Firestore rollups, and an admin analytics tab. That is lightweight analytics engineering inside the app, not a warehouse practice.

## How that work applies to **Analytics Engineer**

Stretch into product-analytics roles at startups without a DE team. Skip dbt/Looker specialist jobs unless you have that stack elsewhere.
