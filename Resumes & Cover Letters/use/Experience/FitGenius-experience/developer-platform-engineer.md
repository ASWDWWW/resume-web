## What this role usually means

Builds internal platforms that make other developers faster: CI templates, environments, paved-path services, documentation portals used by many teams.

## What you actually did on FitGenius

FitGenius is a pre-launch consumer fitness product: iOS and Android (Expo / React Native), Firebase backend (~64 Cloud Functions, Firestore rules, IAP verification, Gemini AI), marketing site (Vite/React), app/legal/admin hosting, store-submission runbooks, and a legal/compliance suite. You were the founding technical partner — one person owning product, code, cloud, QA packets, and founder enablement from roughly May through September 2026.

You built a high-quality *personal/small-team* developer experience: workspace scripts, locale translation pipeline, rules test runner, health-type inventory dump, EAS profiles, mock mode to avoid Firebase bill. The consumer of the platform was essentially you.

## How that work applies to **Developer Platform Engineer**

Stretch into early-stage 'dev platform' jobs at 5–15 person companies. Skip Stripe/Uber-style developer platform orgs.
