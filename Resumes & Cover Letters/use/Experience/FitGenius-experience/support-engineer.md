## What this role usually means

Support engineers debug customer issues, write runbooks, and escalate to product. They need product depth plus patience and ticket hygiene.

## What you actually did on FitGenius

FitGenius is a pre-launch consumer fitness product: iOS and Android (Expo / React Native), Firebase backend (~64 Cloud Functions, Firestore rules, IAP verification, Gemini AI), marketing site (Vite/React), app/legal/admin hosting, store-submission runbooks, and a legal/compliance suite. You were the founding technical partner — one person owning product, code, cloud, QA packets, and founder enablement from roughly May through September 2026.

You built the support surface: public support page, account deletion flow (store requirement), in-app reporting/blocking, staff moderation queue, admin user lookup that *excludes* health/GPS/food logs, incident contact, security logging queries. You have not operated a ticket queue with SLAs.

## How that work applies to **Support Engineer**

You can do Support Engineer work because you understand the product at source level. The gap is volume of customer issues. Good as a bridge role; overkill as a career target given the rest of the repo.
