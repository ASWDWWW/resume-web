## What this role usually means

Product engineers sit with the problem, not just the ticket. They discover what to build, ship it, instrument it, and iterate with users or stakeholders. Titles like this at startups mean full-stack plus product judgment.

## What you actually did on FitGenius

FitGenius is a pre-launch consumer fitness product: iOS and Android (Expo / React Native), Firebase backend (~64 Cloud Functions, Firestore rules, IAP verification, Gemini AI), marketing site (Vite/React), app/legal/admin hosting, store-submission runbooks, and a legal/compliance suite. You were the founding technical partner — one person owning product, code, cloud, QA packets, and founder enablement from roughly May through September 2026.

You wrote the product itself: FEATURES.md, competitive gaps, launch plan, pricing/entitlement matrix, onboarding quiz, living profile, AI coach UX, paywall, founder decision engineering checklist. You translated founder legal decisions (US-only, no ads, 18+ gate, Gemini audit) into code. You packaged asked-vs-shipped evidence for a non-technical founder.

## How that work applies to **Product Engineer**

This title is a near-perfect description of the FitGenius role. Interviews will ask 'how did you decide what to build?' — you have written artifacts for that, not only code.
