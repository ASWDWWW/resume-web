## What this role usually means

Solutions engineers win and expand customers by mapping product to a customer's problem, running demos/POCs, writing integration guides, and unblocking technical objections. Mix of sales-engineering and implementation.

## What you actually did on FitGenius

FitGenius is a pre-launch consumer fitness product: iOS and Android (Expo / React Native), Firebase backend (~64 Cloud Functions, Firestore rules, IAP verification, Gemini AI), marketing site (Vite/React), app/legal/admin hosting, store-submission runbooks, and a legal/compliance suite. You were the founding technical partner — one person owning product, code, cloud, QA packets, and founder enablement from roughly May through September 2026.

Your 'customer' was the founder and, later, App Review. You produced status packets, asked-vs-shipped reports, Android emulator screenshot packs, store paste sheets, legal review emails, IAP visual QA, GPS QA scripts, and an admin portal so a non-engineer can operate moderation/analytics. That is solutions work even though it is not a named SE org.

## How that work applies to **Solutions Engineer**

Directly transferable to SE: translate technical reality for a non-technical buyer, produce evidence, run the product in their context (simulator/device QA), and document how to operate it. You lack a quota-carrying sales motion and multi-customer variety.
