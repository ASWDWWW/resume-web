## What this role typically requires

Engineers who own outcomes: talk to users/stakeholders, pick what to build, ship the feature, measure whether it worked. Mix of PM taste and IC execution. Common at startups.

## How that work applies to Product Engineer

Product Engineers are judged on judgment plus shipping. The mobile dock is a product decision. Prominent 988/911 is a product + ethics decision. `llms.txt` and IndexNow are product bets on future discovery. Parallel redesign sandbox is a product-risk decision. This is closer to founding-engineer-for-a-clinic than to a ticket-taker.

## Gaps versus a typical hiring bar

No instrumentation (analytics, funnels, A/B). No patient portal or clinical workflow product. “Product Engineer” at a SaaS company still expects more application logic. You have product sense on a marketing surface.

## How to talk about it

Tell the story as “I was the product engineer for a healthcare practice’s public product.” Walk one decision that said no (reviews) and one that reduced risk (redesign sandbox). Admit you lack product analytics in this codebase.
