## What this job title usually means

Solution architects design how a vendor’s (or enterprise’s) systems fit a customer landscape: integration maps, security reviews, multi-system diagrams, sometimes executive communication.

## What you actually did in this project

You architected a small SaaS: clients, BaaS, billing providers (Stripe + stores), security rules, analytics. You designed and implemented both clients and the Firebase/Functions backend so one user account, one Firestore model, and one billing state work on web and mobile. That is solution architecture at product scale, not enterprise landscape scale.

## How that work applies to this title

You can diagram a coherent system and defend choices (webhook entitlements, custom claims vs emails in rules). That is the architect muscle.

## Gaps (be ready to say these out loud)

No multi-account enterprise integrations, no TOGAF-style artifacts, no customer environment constraints (their SSO, their VPC). 'Architect' titles often expect that breadth or seniority.

## Interview / resume angle

Bring a one-page architecture diagram. Do not claim 'enterprise solution architect' from a single Firebase app.

## Verdict

Partial. Better as Solutions Engineer or Product Engineer than SA unless the SA job is startup-sized.
