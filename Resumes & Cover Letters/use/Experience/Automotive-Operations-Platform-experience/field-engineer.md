## What this role is hired to do

Field engineers go on-site (or to a customer environment) to install, configure, and fix systems — often hardware, networks, or on-prem software.

## What you actually built

Alex Road Service is a production-shaped **marketing website plus internal shop operations platform** for a 24/7 commercial roadside and truck repair business in Keasbey, NJ. You were the **sole builder** from first commit (March 2026) through repo split (September 2026): public site, 20+ ops screens, Firebase Auth/Firestore/Functions/Storage/Hosting, Stripe Checkout with webhook-recorded payments and refunds, RBAC, encrypted staff messaging, VIN decode, i18n, CI/CD, security rules tests, runbooks, and a LaunchPage Studios closed-sales / delivery packet so the same platform can be duplicated for other shops.

## How that work maps to Field Engineer

Your “field” work is product design for *their* field technicians (status updates, photos) and a delivery model that could include on-site training. You are not evidenced as someone who racks servers or commissions devices at customer sites.

## Concrete evidence from this project

- Technician-centric WO flows and media capture.
- Kickoff, training agenda, and staging access documents.
- Post-sale process that can include in-person reviews (50%/100%).

## Gaps versus a typical hiring bar

No on-site install history, no hardware, no customer-environment networking. Weak match unless the company uses “field” to mean forward deployed (see FDE docs).
