# Legal preparation, privacy, security, compliance

Command: `/legal-prep`. Subagent: `security-privacy`.

This workspace supports research and professional handoffs. Generated documents are **not** legal approval or verified compliance.

## Records

Contracts, obligations, renewals, retention, access reviews, licenses: store pointers here and full text only if the user provides it. Prefer `ops/restricted/` for sensitive copies, knowing gitignore ≠ ACL.

## Data handling

- No PHI, payroll, or live customer dumps in this public repo.
- Career packets may mention clinic systems; they are local notes, not product canon (see BariAccess README).
- Retention: **unresolved**. Default: do not delete user content during OS work.

## Workflows

Vendor assessment template: `ops/templates/vendor-assessment.md`.  
Evidence collection: date, source, hash/path, what it proves, what it does not prove.

Current applicability research must cite the jurisdiction and date. Handoff to a human professional before relying on it.
