## What the trip was

Bariatric Associates is building **BariAccess** — a digital health platform that keeps bariatric patients connected to care after they leave the clinic. The post’s one-line product definition is the one to keep: the layer between a world-class medical visit and everything that happens in a patient’s life between appointments.

To grow that vision, Dr. Andrei and Costin Peiu brought me to Belgrade to meet PROFEX Academy, one of Europe’s most advanced human-performance centers, and explore a partnership that could make them one of BariAccess’s first clients.

The trip was four days of:

1. Business meetings
2. Live BariAccess platform demos
3. Hands-on testing — I went through PROFEX’s full diagnostic protocol myself, right off a nine-hour flight

That last piece is the product insight. I left the lab loaded with data and no digital layer to carry it forward. That gap became the product brief.

## Who PROFEX is

[PROFEX Academy](https://profexacademy.com/en/) is a sports-diagnostic institution in New Belgrade, not a medical clinic. Their own site is explicit: employees are not medical professionals. The backbone of the work is a unique training method delivered as individual programs, based on each client’s needs and medical history.

Public facts that match the post:

- **~25 years** as a “healthy life academy”; LinkedIn company page lists founded **2012**, HQ Belgrade, 11–50 employees
- Diagnostics happen in their sports laboratory. Testing takes several hours. Results come back as a written report (text, tables, graphs) plus a scheduled walkthrough
- CEO and founder: **Duško Ilić, PhD** — full professor of biomechanics and motor control at the University of Belgrade; vice-dean of the Faculty of Sports and Physical Education; creator of the MDI© Method (Manual Duško Ilić). Over 30 years of experience, patents, and 30+ published papers. The post calls him Professor Dusko
- Diagnostics are run under the supervision of two biomechanics professors: **Prof. Duško Ilić** and **Prof. Vladimir Mrdaković**. Do not claim Prof. Mrdaković was in the partnership meetings unless a source names him there
- Repeat testing is part of their model (segments or full batteries every 3, 7, 14, 30, or 60 days). That cadence is why a between-visit digital layer is the actual product problem

The professional diagnostic package on their site is 2.5–3.5 hours of examination plus about 60 minutes talking through results. That is the protocol I sat through on day one.

## Day-one protocol, mapped to PROFEX’s published tests

I was the subject, not the clinician. PROFEX ran the tests. The list below is the post’s protocol matched to what PROFEX publishes, so the language stays specific without inventing equipment.

| What the post says | PROFEX published diagnostic | Equipment / method (from PROFEX) |
|---|---|---|
| InBody composition scan | [Analysis of a body structure](https://profexacademy.com/en/diagnostics/analysis-of-a-body-structure/) | Biospace (InBody) Body 770 / Body 720; 6 frequencies across 5 body segments; fat/muscle, water, proteins, minerals, left–right split |
| Posture imaging | [Postural / spinal-column analysis](https://profexacademy.com/en/diagnostics/analysis-of-the-status-of-the-spinal-column-postural-analysis-analysis-of-body-posture/) | TEMPLO software with up to 12 high-speed cameras; 2D/3D postural protocols; often paired with a pressure plate for a 4D view of stance |
| Foot pressure analysis | [Foot function, statics and dynamics](https://profexacademy.com/en/diagnostics/analysis-of-foot-function-statics-and-dynamics-of-foot-function/) | AmCube FootWork Pro / GaitScan; 400+ pressure sensors; up to 300 readings per second |
| EMG muscle testing | [Telemetric kinesiology EMG](https://profexacademy.com/en/diagnostics/telemetric-kinesiology-electromyography-analysis-emg/) | Delsys Trigno, 16 wireless channels; postural and sport-specific muscle tests |
| VO2 max treadmill stress test | [Cardiorespiratory function analysis](https://profexacademy.com/en/diagnostics/cardiorespiratory-function-analysis/) | Cosmed Fitmate Pro; VO2 max, resting metabolic rate, anaerobic threshold, training zones |
| Isokinetic strength testing | [Isokinetic dynamometric neuromuscular testing](https://profexacademy.com/en/diagnostics/isokinetic-dynamometric-neuromuscular-testing/) | Human Norm dynamometer (CSMI Medical Solutions), in use since 2020; PROFEX says they are the only institution in Serbia doing this testing, training, and rehab |
| Isometric testing | Same dynamometer + EMG MVC | Human Norm isometric regime (muscle length unchanged); EMG MVC as isometric contraction |
| Full EKG | Named in the post; not a standalone page on the English diagnostics list | Keep as “full EKG” from the post. Do not invent a device name. Cardio work on site is framed around Cosmed Fitmate Pro |
| Anthropometric measurements | [Morphological testing](https://profexacademy.com/en/diagnostics/morphological-testing/) | Lengths and volumes of body parts; pelvic alignment; joint range-of-motion (amplitude) |

That battery is the “professional” diagnostic track, not the shorter basic or pediatric packages.
