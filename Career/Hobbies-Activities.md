# Hobbies & Activities

- Calisthenics. I train with bodyweight movements like pull-ups, push-ups, dips, handstands, and core work. It is how I stay strong and mobile without needing a full gym.

- Indoor workouts (Gym). I lift and do structured gym sessions for strength, conditioning, and recovery alongside football and other sports.

- Outdoor workouts (running). I run outside for cardio and to clear my head. Roads, trails, and track work keep training from staying stuck indoors.

- Basketball. I play pickup and work on handles, shooting, and defense. It is fast, competitive, and a different kind of athleticism from football.

- Music Creation (Pop & R&B). I write and produce pop and R&B. Making tracks is how I create when I am not building software or training.

- Golf. I play to work on patience, focus, and a repeatable swing. It is slower than the rest of my sports and I like that contrast.

- Snowboarding. I snowboard when I can get on a mountain. Balance, timing, and committing to a line are the parts that click for me.

- Hiking. I hike to get outside, cover ground, and stay in the landscape instead of just passing through it. Trails are another kind of outdoor workout, slower than a run and longer than a gym session.

- Traveling. I like being in new places and learning how people live there. Studying abroad in Townsville, Australia made that a real habit, not just a weekend trip.

- Cooking. I cook my own meals and like figuring out a dish from start to finish. It is practical, creative, and another way I take care of myself.

- Gun Range / Skeet Shooting. I shoot at the range and do skeet for focus, safety, and a steady hand. It is a skill sport more than a gym sport.

- Journaling. I write to think things through, keep track of what I am working on, and stay honest with myself about how a week actually went.

- Poker. I play for the reads, the math, and the decisions under pressure. It is a game of patience and timing more than luck.
