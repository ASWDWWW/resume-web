## What this job title usually means

Infrastructure engineers own servers, networks, capacity, IaC, reliability of the substrate other apps run on.

## What you actually did in this project

You consumed managed infrastructure (Firebase/GCP, Stripe, EAS, Expo push, AdMob) and configured it: regions `us-central1`, hosting headers, Cloud Functions runtime Node 20, scheduled functions, storage buckets, indexes JSON.

## How that work applies to this title

Selecting and configuring managed infra is real, but it is application-infra, not infrastructure engineering as a discipline.

## Gaps (be ready to say these out loud)

No Terraform/Pulumi, no load balancers you designed, no capacity planning, no VM/network topology. Hosting is a static SPA + Functions.

## Interview / resume angle
