## How that work applies to Mobile Engineer

Mobile Engineers care about thumbs, safe areas, and tap reliability. You actually sweat those on the web. A PWA-flavored healthcare site is adjacent to “mobile product,” and many companies still hire “mobile” people who start with responsive web.

Position as mobile web / responsive specialist. Do not claim iOS/Android engineering. If targeting RN jobs, this is UI taste + product sense, not platform skill.
