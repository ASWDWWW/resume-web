# Solutions Engineer

**Project:** Alex Road Service (LaunchPage Studios)  
**Evidence fit:** **8.6 / 10** · **Band:** Strong match  
**How to use this in hiring:** Ready to use this as a flagship example

> This score measures how strongly *this repository* maps to a typical **Solutions Engineer** hiring bar. It is not years of experience, a compensation band, or a prediction that you would receive an offer.

## What this role is hired to do

Solutions Engineers translate a product into a customer’s world: discovery, demos, technical objections, POCs, and a path to production. They are technical enough to be credible and commercial enough to close and expand.

## What you actually built

Alex Road Service is a production-shaped **marketing website plus internal shop operations platform** for a 24/7 commercial roadside and truck repair business in Keasbey, NJ. You were the **sole builder** from first commit (March 2026) through repo split (September 2026): public site, 20+ ops screens, Firebase Auth/Firestore/Functions/Storage/Hosting, Stripe Checkout with webhook-recorded payments and refunds, RBAC, encrypted staff messaging, VIN decode, i18n, CI/CD, security rules tests, runbooks, and a LaunchPage Studios closed-sales / delivery packet so the same platform can be duplicated for other shops.

## How that work maps to Solutions Engineer

LaunchPage Studios *is* a solutions motion. You built the reference platform, a live demo, role-specific walkthrough accounts, Stripe test-card guidance, a sales/marketing brief with objection handling, pricing packages (website / platform / full stack; Essentials / Growth / Complete), and a ten-stage post-sale delivery machine. That is solutions engineering plus the unusual extra that you also *wrote the product being sold*.

## Concrete evidence from this project

- Live demo: `https://launchpage-alex-roadservice.web.app/` with staff login path.
- Sales brief: elevator pitches, competitive angles, do’s/don’ts, proof points.
- Closed-sales documents: NDA, MSA, SOW, feature checklist, billing, Stripe acknowledgement, UAT script, training agenda, hypercare.
- Delivery playbook: start gates, 50%/100% reviews, change orders, retainer rules that protect IP.

## Gaps versus a typical hiring bar

You may not have a long log of named enterprise deals, RFPs, or multi-stakeholder procurement. Evidence is that you *built the system a Solutions Engineer would use*, and the process around one flagship customer/vertical, not 20 logged POCs.

## How to talk about this

- Lead with: “I built the product and the sales-engineering system around it.”
- Demo the office vs technician role live — that is classic SE discovery.
- Explain how you keep the platform licensed, not sold as source.
