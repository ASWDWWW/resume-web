> This score measures how strongly *this repository* maps to a typical **Software Generalist** hiring bar. It is not years of experience, a compensation band, or a prediction that you would receive an offer.

## What this role is hired to do

Software generalists (common at startups and studios) wear many hats: product, frontend, backend, cloud, security-enough, docs, and customer delivery. Depth in one layer can be lower if breadth ships the company.

## What you actually built

Alex Road Service is a production-shaped **marketing website plus internal shop operations platform** for a 24/7 commercial roadside and truck repair business in Keasbey, NJ. You were the **sole builder** from first commit (March 2026) through repo split (September 2026): public site, 20+ ops screens, Firebase Auth/Firestore/Functions/Storage/Hosting, Stripe Checkout with webhook-recorded payments and refunds, RBAC, encrypted staff messaging, VIN decode, i18n, CI/CD, security rules tests, runbooks, and a LaunchPage Studios closed-sales / delivery packet so the same platform can be duplicated for other shops.

## How that work maps to Software Generalist

This is the strongest natural title for the work. One person specified the domain, designed the system, implemented UI and Functions, wrote rules and tests, integrated Stripe, set up CI, produced legal/sales/delivery documents, and prepared a duplicate-and-customize studio motion. That is generalist excellence.

## Concrete evidence from this project

- Every major layer of the repo: `dev/public`, `dev/functions`, rules, tests, CI, business packet.
- Security, payments, i18n, media, scheduling, HR-lite, marketing site.
- Sales enablement and post-sale operations, not only code.

## Gaps versus a typical hiring bar

Generalist can be read as “not deep.” Counter with one deep slice (payments + RBAC). Large companies may still bin you into a specialist ladder after hiring.
