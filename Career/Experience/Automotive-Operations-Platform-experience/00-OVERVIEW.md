3. **Forward Deployed is real if the company means customer-embedded software.** It is not real for clearance, hospitality domain, or AI/agentic SDLC specialties.
4. **AI/ML/embedded/native mobile/hardware/networking are not evidenced.** Listing them from this repo would be a credibility problem.
5. **SWE II / SDE II / Senior FDE are stretch-up, not stretch-down.** You have the ownership shape; you lack team scale, multi-customer deploy count, and mainstream large-org stack proof.
6. **Staff / Principal / Lead (people leadership) are documents-ahead-of-scope.** The playbooks are impressive; the organizational mandate is not shown.
