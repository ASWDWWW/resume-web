# Forward Deployed Engineer (FDE)

## What this role usually means

FDEs sit with a customer, learn their workflow, and ship software in *their* context — often the company's product plus glue, enablement, and feedback to product. Palantir-origin title now used across AI companies.

## What you actually did on FitGenius

FitGenius is a pre-launch consumer fitness product: iOS and Android (Expo / React Native), Firebase backend (~64 Cloud Functions, Firestore rules, IAP verification, Gemini AI), marketing site (Vite/React), app/legal/admin hosting, store-submission runbooks, and a legal/compliance suite. You were the founding technical partner — one person owning product, code, cloud, QA packets, and founder enablement from roughly May through September 2026.

You were embedded with a single 'customer' (FitGenius LLC founder): you translated business/legal decisions into software, delivered in their timeline (phase exhibits), produced operator packets, said NO-GO with evidence, and built admin tools so they could operate without you in the app binary. That is FDE motion on one account.

## How that work applies to **Forward Deployed Engineer (FDE)**

Use FitGenius as an FDE origin story: stakeholder embedding, delivery under constraints, enablement artifacts, product feedback loop. You lack multi-customer deployments, travel, and enterprise environment integration (VPC, SSO, on-prem).

## Strengths a hiring manager can credit

- Embedded with a non-technical principal
- Delivery + honesty when not launch-ready
- Enablement and ops surfaces
- Full-stack ability to actually ship the glue

## Gaps versus a typical hiring bar

- One account
- Customer environment was your own cloud, not theirs
- No enterprise deployment patterns

## How to talk about it

Do not claim Palantir-style FDE. Claim 'founding FDE for a consumer product company.'
