## What this role usually means

Senior FDE owns an account technically, unblocks others, sets delivery strategy, and handles messy enterprise constraints without daily supervision.

## What you actually did on FitGenius

FitGenius is a pre-launch consumer fitness product: iOS and Android (Expo / React Native), Firebase backend (~64 Cloud Functions, Firestore rules, IAP verification, Gemini AI), marketing site (Vite/React), app/legal/admin hosting, store-submission runbooks, and a legal/compliance suite. You were the founding technical partner — one person owning product, code, cloud, QA packets, and founder enablement from roughly May through September 2026.

You owned the *only* account completely — which looks senior in autonomy — but you did not run multiple deployments, coach other FDEs, or integrate into a customer's IT estate.

## How that work applies to **Senior Forward Deployed Engineer**

Stretch. Autonomy is senior; customer complexity is not. Apply if the company counts founding-engineer embedding as senior FDE. Expect interviewers to probe enterprise stories you do not have.
