## What this role usually means

Enterprise wording for someone who builds business applications (UI + APIs + data) rather than compilers or infrastructure. Often Java/.NET in postings, but the *work* is product software.

## What you actually did on FitGenius

FitGenius is a pre-launch consumer fitness product: iOS and Android (Expo / React Native), Firebase backend (~64 Cloud Functions, Firestore rules, IAP verification, Gemini AI), marketing site (Vite/React), app/legal/admin hosting, store-submission runbooks, and a legal/compliance suite. You were the founding technical partner — one person owning product, code, cloud, QA packets, and founder enablement from roughly May through September 2026.

You built the application layer of FitGenius: user-facing mobile app, admin portal, support/legal sites, Firestore data model, and callable APIs. Features are real business workflows — onboarding, subscriptions, moderation, account deletion — not demos.

## How that work applies to **Application Developer**

If the posting wants 'applications that users run every day,' this is a direct map. If it requires a specific enterprise stack (SAP, Salesforce Apex, Java Spring shops), FitGenius is only transferable reasoning, not stack match. Keep the resume product-shaped: screens, APIs, data, authZ.
