## What this role usually means

A general software engineer designs, builds, tests, and ships production software across one or more layers of a product. Hiring bars usually look for ownership of features end-to-end, clean code, reviews/tests, and the ability to debug real systems.

## What you actually did on FitGenius

FitGenius is a pre-launch consumer fitness product: iOS and Android (Expo / React Native), Firebase backend (~64 Cloud Functions, Firestore rules, IAP verification, Gemini AI), marketing site (Vite/React), app/legal/admin hosting, store-submission runbooks, and a legal/compliance suite. You were the founding technical partner — one person owning product, code, cloud, QA packets, and founder enablement from roughly May through September 2026.

You designed and implemented a full consumer product: 65+ mobile screens, a Firebase backend with callable APIs, Firestore triggers, scheduled jobs, a Vite marketing site, a staff admin portal, CI, EAS builds, security rules, and founder-facing launch documentation. The work covers auth, payments (IAP), AI, health platforms, GPS, social, moderation, analytics, and store-readiness — not a tutorial app.

## How that work applies to **Software Engineer**

This is the default title for what you already do. Interviewers for Software Engineer want proof you can take an ambiguous product problem, break it into systems, ship it, and keep it maintainable. FitGenius is that proof at product scale: typed domain models, tests, CI, entitlements, and production cloud, even though public launch is still gated.
