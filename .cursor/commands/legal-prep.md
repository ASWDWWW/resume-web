# Legal and compliance preparation

Read `ops/business-operations/legal.md`.

Prepare research, checklists, contract summaries, retention notes, or vendor questions. Cite sources and dates.

Do not present output as legal advice, approval, or verified compliance.

Handoff to a professional using `ops/templates/vendor-assessment.md` or a dated brief. Sensitive records go under `ops/restricted/` only if the user pastes them; remind that the folder is not a permission boundary.
