# Follow up on open work

Use `ops/CONTROL-CENTER.md`, open tickets, and dated records — not chat memory.

1. List active work, owners, due dates, and blockers.
2. Retrieve evidence for each follow-up (ticket, decision, email draft, meeting note). Keep attribution.
3. Draft follow-ups. Do not send mail or messages unless the user explicitly asked and the sending identity is confirmed in `ops/integrations/ROUTING.md`.
4. Match approved writing examples in `ops/business-operations/communications.md` when they exist.
5. Update obligations and the control center.

Separate coworker, customer, executive, and investor audiences.
