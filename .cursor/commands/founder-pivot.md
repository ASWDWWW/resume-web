# Founder pivot

Preserve existing work. Do not delete career packets, content, or unfinished resume files.

1. Record the new direction in `ops/profile/PROJECT-PROFILE.md` with date, confirmed vs assumed.
2. Mark superseded requirements in tickets and workflows. Do not erase them.
3. Update `ops/CONTROL-CENTER.md` goals, owners, and success metrics.
4. Redirect affected commands, skills, and agents by editing their files; do not rely on chat.
5. List automations that must be disabled to prevent acting on old goals.
6. Report what stayed, what is superseded, and what now needs a decision.

Use `ops/templates/pivot.md`.
