# Founder pivot

1. Snapshot current goals from `ops/CONTROL-CENTER.md` into the pivot record.
2. Preserve career packets, content, and unfinished resume work. Do not delete.
3. Write `ops/templates/pivot.md` filled in under `ops/records/` with date, new direction, superseded items, and affected automations.
4. Update project profile (confirmed vs assumed), control center, and routing only if identities change.
5. Disable automations that would act on old goals (`ops/automations/CATALOG.md`).
6. Point skills and commands at the new sources of truth; do not rely on chat.

Do not contact customers or investors as part of the pivot unless asked.
